## §1 · Master business logic

**One line:** *Enterprises are deploying AI agents faster than they can prove what those agents did. BIZRA sells the proof — consent, receipts, and replayable evidence for AI work — as a local-first product that never sees your data.*

The chain of logic:
1. Agents act → regulators, auditors, and courts demand evidence of what they did (EU AI Act Art. 12; ISO/IEC 42001; NIST AI RMF; GDPR adjacency).
2. Ordinary logs are mutable → evidentiary value ≈ zero. Compliance-grade = signed, chained, tamper-evident, replayable.
3. Cloud observability vendors require sending data out → unacceptable for banks, government, defense, health. **Local-first is the differentiator, not a limitation.**
4. BIZRA is the only entrant whose engine is simultaneously: local-first · zero-dependency (supply-chain clean) · consent-gated (human-in-the-loop by construction) · signed + hash-chained (Ed25519/BLAKE3/SHA-256) · values-native (RIBA_ZERO, consent, transparency — direct alignment with Islamic-finance governance).
5. Therefore: **sell trust, not tokens.** Red Hat logic — open substrate, paid verification, support, and sovereignty. Proof traces are the product.

**Moats (defensible, in order):** the receipt/isnad architecture with three years of receipts behind it · zero-dep local-first posture · the founder provenance corpus · GCC values-alignment no Western vendor can credibly copy · cross-AI consent discipline already demonstrated (GPT proposed, Codex obeyed the gate, Claude audited — one constitution, three vendors).

## §3 · Master business model

**Type:** open-core + services-led entry → subscription core → enterprise licenses. Fiat only through 2026; the token layer is architecture, not revenue, until PoI + legal rails exist.

**Revenue mix targets:** H2-2026: ~70% services (Proof Packs) / 30% subscriptions. 2027: flip toward subscriptions + first enterprise.
**Unit economics (solo-founder era):** cost base ≈ living + infra. Break-even ≈ **2 Proof Packs per quarter** or ~100 Pro seats. This is the most capital-efficient shape available: no fundraise required to reach ramen-profitable.
**Pricing philosophy:** value-anchored to incident and penalty cost (one unproven AI action in a regulated workflow costs more than a year of BIZRA), not to hours.
**The 50% community pledge:** honored as the founder's personal distribution oath at the entity level — communicated as covenant, never sold as protocol yield (that framing would violate both securities caution and RIBA_ZERO).
**Scenario (not forecast), H2-2026 conservative:** 3 pilots × $10k + early Pro seats ≈ **$35–50k**. Enough to prove the Economic pillar with receipts, which is the actual goal.
