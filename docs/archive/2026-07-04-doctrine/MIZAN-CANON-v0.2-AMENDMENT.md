## 2 · Reverse Self-Improvement — the founder's word, kept

> **Improvement is never promised forward from projections. It is only proven backward from receipts.**
> A single Mizan grade is a snapshot. A *series* of grades on recountable receipts is a derivative — and the derivative, not the self-report, is the only legal reward signal.
