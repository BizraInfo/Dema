MITHAQ — Seven Laws القوانين السبعة

one per pillar · v0.1
01
المعنىMeaning
Every pixel states a fact or asks a consent. Nothing decorative survives review. No stock metaphor, no filler copy, no chart without a receipt behind it.
02
الخشوعHumility
The system opens with its limits. CURRENT_LIMITS is the splash screen. Confidence appears only where proof exists; everywhere else, restraint.
03
البرهانProof
Color is evidence. Only MEASURED may wear gold and glow. LOCAL_ONLY is moonlight. DESIGNED_NOT_LIVE is an outline. BLOCKED is honest ember. Nothing unearned shines.
04
الإحسانIhsān
Restraint is the spectacle. One motion per truth-event: the seed breathes because the node is alive; a ring closes because a receipt sealed. Nothing else moves.
05
السيادةSovereignty
The largest element on any screen is the human's next decision — never the system's output, never its self-praise. The interface serves the hand that consents.
06
النماءGrowth
The chain grows; it is never drawn ahead of time. Sealed days are permanent, today breathes, future days are ghosts. Progress is shown as receipts, not projections.
07
الرحمةMercy
A refusal is gentle and complete: what it won't do, why, and what remains yours. Errors never apologize and never blur. Fail-closed, kindly.
