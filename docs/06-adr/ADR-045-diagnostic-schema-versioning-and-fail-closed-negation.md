## Decision

Two rules govern any deterministic diagnostic/verifier kernel whose schema is
hardened over time (established while hardening `DEMA-FDE-DUAL-DIAGNOSTIC-1A`
through PR #396, squash-merged to `main` as `816cd31`):

1. **A schema label never grants historical provenance.** When a report schema
   is bumped (`v0.1 → v0.2`) to bind a stronger policy, historical reports are
   verified by **re-deriving the whole diagnosis under a frozen reconstruction
   of the prior algorithm** and requiring the carried hash to match. Relabeling
   a current report as the legacy schema and rehashing it is rejected, because
   the fabricated classification is not what the frozen prior algorithm derives
   from the same input. Historical reports remain `authority_eligible: false`.

2. **Ambiguous evidence fails closed, and that is by design — not a defect to
   drive to zero.** A diagnostic classifier consumes machine-emitted output; any
   natural-language matching (e.g. negation handling) is a *bounded best-effort
   heuristic*, never a parser. When boundary evidence is genuinely ambiguous the
   classifier resolves to the **hard-stop class** (`boundary_violation`), which
   escalates to a human at `authority_delta 0`. Over-classification costs a
   review; it can never launder authority. This is the authority-monotonicity
   law applied to uncertainty: an unclear signal may only preserve or reduce
   authority, never increase it.

## Context

Hardening a shipped classifier created two recurring hazards, both surfaced by
independent review of PR #396:

- **Provenance laundering.** The first legacy path checked structure + hash
  only. A rejected `v0.2` report could be relabeled `v0.1`, rehashed, and
  accepted as historical evidence — a fabricated classification entering the
  proof surface. Frozen semantic rederivation closes this. Authenticity of the
  frozen algorithm is pinned by a fixture and a 17-vector corpus generated by
  the actual parent-commit module (hash-identical), spanning every failure
  class, measured status, and confidence tier.

- **An unbounded precision chase.** A regex negation heuristic over free text has
  an infinite tail; a live review harness will keep generating prose edges each
  time the head changes. Three such edges were found and fixed, but the durable
  answer is a *contract*, not more regexes: FDE classifies machine output, its
  negation is bounded best-effort, and residual ambiguity fails closed. This
  converts an open-ended chase into a principled, safe spec.

## Consequences

- Future schema bumps of any diagnostic/verifier kernel follow rule 1: keep the
  prior algorithm frozen and rederive; do not trust a schema string. Pin the
  frozen algorithm with an authentic corpus, not a single fixture.
- Reviewers' natural-language precision findings on a machine-output classifier
  are quality refinements, resolvable on principle (fail-closed by design), not
  merge blockers — provided the fail-closed direction is proven (over-classify,
  never under-classify a real violation).
- Truth labels unchanged: the FDE surfaces remain `PREVIEW_ONLY`; this ADR adds
  no runtime, no authority, no economic claim. `authority_delta: 0`.

## What this does not decide

- It does not make FDE a natural-language parser, nor promise exhaustive prose
  handling.
- It does not authorize any runtime, mint, federation, or economic surface.
- It does not apply to non-deterministic or model-backed classifiers, which
  carry their own provenance and calibration concerns out of this scope.
