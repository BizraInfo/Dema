## 1. Purpose

This document is the **claim-governed narrative blueprint** for BIZRA's first public face in 2026. It is not a campaign launch. It is not a video. It is not Canva work. It is the canonical text that every future First-Look artifact (deck, video script, landing page, lighthouse invitation, social opener, Canva slide, visual-emulator caption) **must** consult and obey.

The intended reader is any future contributor — designer, narrator, marketer, copywriter, video producer — who will turn the seven-pillar canon already on `main` into a public-facing artifact. This doc tells them **what they may say, what they must show, what they may hint, and what is forbidden**.

## 10. Narrative Spine

The First-Look narrative arc is a concise 10-beat sequence inherited from the seven-pillar canon:

1. Humanity has always asked who we are and why we are here.
2. BIZRA began as an Arabic root-paper seed: `الرسالة` and `البذرة`.
3. The first cinematic expression said BIZRA was about *choice, goodness, solidarity, kindness, and support*.
4. One human then carried the seed into code, docs, receipts, Node0, and Dema.
5. Node0 is the living local proof: one human, one node, one task, inspectable and improvable.
6. Dema is the product face / home base for the user's local node.
7. PAT remains local as the user's personal think tank and task force.
8. SAT / UKE / URP are the designed forest/soil layer, **not yet public-live runtime**.
9. The invitation is not "believe the founder." The invitation is **"inspect, test, improve, surpass."**
10. Every human may choose to become a node/seed without surrendering dignity, consent, or memory.

This spine is the load-bearing structure under every scene below. A First-Look artifact that loses any of these beats has drifted from the canon.
