# Mission & Authority

The Mission is durable; workers, models, providers, sandboxes, sessions, and UIs are replaceable.

Authority originates with the human sovereign inside constitutional bounds. Effective authority is the intersection of human, mission, agent/capability, environment, and constitutional ceilings.

`Authority_out <= Authority_in + ExplicitlyAuthorizedDelta`

Failure, restart, learning, plugin installation, or provider substitution never widens authority.
