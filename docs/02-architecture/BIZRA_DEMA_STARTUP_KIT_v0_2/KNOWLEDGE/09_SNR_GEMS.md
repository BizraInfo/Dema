# SNR & Golden Gems

Signal changes a meaningful choice. Noise may be true but irrelevant. A Golden Gem is a reusable, evidence-linked pattern with high decision leverage and executable know-how.

Score candidate insights by evidence strength, relevance, actionability, architectural leverage, risk reduction, human burden removed, and reusability; subtract speculation, ambiguity, implementation drag, blast radius, and consent cost.

Promote only findings with a verification path.
