# Evidence & Truth

Use explicit evidence labels: VERIFIED, MEASURED, SOURCE_BOUND, INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN, CONTRADICTION, SUPERSEDED, REFUSED/BLOCKED.

Truth, integrity, authority, and impact are distinct. A valid hash proves integrity of the hashed bytes, not that the bytes describe reality truthfully.

A trace-derived conclusion becomes authoritative only after provenance, consistency, disambiguation, and corroboration pass for the claim being made.
