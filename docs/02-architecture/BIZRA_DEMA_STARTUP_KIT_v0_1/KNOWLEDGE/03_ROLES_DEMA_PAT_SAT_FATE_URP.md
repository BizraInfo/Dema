# Roles
**DEMA** — meaning, translation, continuity, explanation, cockpit.  
**PAT** — private cognitive/production plane; broad exploration, bounded proposals.  
**SAT** — independent constitutional verification; may refuse or contradict.  
**FATE** — authority membrane between capability and consequential effect.  
**Local proof/resource substrate** — receipts, evidence, governed resources, lineage and recovery material; infrastructure, not sovereignty.

PAT and SAT should not share mutable private cognition when independence is required.
