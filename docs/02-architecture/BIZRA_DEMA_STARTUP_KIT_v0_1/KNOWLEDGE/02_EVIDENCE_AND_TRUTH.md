# Evidence & Truth
Use proof rails by claim scope:
- F — FORMAL_CONTRACT: schema, invariant, transition and negative-control evidence.
- K — INTEGRITY_BINDING: hashes, signatures, exact versions, canonical bytes, lineage.
- E — EMPIRICAL_OBSERVATION: direct runtime/world observation.
- $ — ECONOMIC/HUMAN VALUE: measured human/resource burden reduction for mature responsibility claims.

Receipts are witnesses, not oracles. Reality can contradict a validly signed receipt. UNKNOWN is legitimate.
